## About the Founder

Pandit Satish Chandra Srivastava, born on 8th September 1938 in Allahabad, Uttar Pradesh, was a distinguished sitarist and music educator. He began his sitar training under Prof. Banwari Lal and, from 1963, honed his craft under the tutelage of Sitar maestro Pt. Ravi Shankar.

His academic career spanned nearly four decades as a faculty member and later Head of the Department of Music at D.G.P.G. College, Kanpur (1960–1999). Recognized as a "Top Grade Artist" by All India Radio and Doordarshan, he performed on prestigious stages worldwide and conducted numerous workshops to promote Hindustani Classical Music.

Honoured with titles such as **Sangeetacharya**, **Sangeet Kala Ratna**, **Sangeet Shiromani**, and the **Sangeet Natak Akademi Award**, his contributions were also featured in esteemed journals like *Sangeet Kala Vihar* and *Sangeet*. He authored numerous books to pass on his knowledge for the benefit of future generations.

In 2024, a research thesis titled *"Pandit Satish Chandra Srivastava Ka Sangeet Ke Kshetra Mein Yogdan"* was documented at Punjab University, highlighting his significant impact on Indian Classical Music.

## About Us

At Sangeet Shree Prakashan, we are passionate about preserving and promoting the rich tradition of Hindustani Classical Music through authentic, syllabus-oriented publications. Established with a deep respect for India's musical heritage, our goal is to make classical music education accessible, simple, and enjoyable for students, teachers, and enthusiasts alike.

We specialize in publishing music books in languages like Hindi and English, crafted meticulously to align with academic syllabuses, yet designed to foster a genuine appreciation of the art form beyond examinations. Every book we publish is a reflection of our commitment to authenticity, quality, and cultural responsibility.

Our mission goes beyond just providing study material. We aim to bridge the gap between traditional music knowledge and modern learning needs, ensuring that learners not only acquire technical proficiency but also understand the spiritual depth and beauty of Hindustani Classical Music.

Over the years, Sangeet Shree Prakashan has earned the trust of students, educators, and institutions as a reliable source for well-structured, easy-to-understand music books. Our vision is to nurture a new generation of musicians who cherish classical music as a lifelong passion, not merely a subject for exams. We believe that music education should be an enriching journey—one that connects learners to India's rich cultural roots while preparing them for academic excellence.

Join us in our mission to keep the timeless art of Hindustani Classical Music alive, accessible, and thriving for generations to come.
